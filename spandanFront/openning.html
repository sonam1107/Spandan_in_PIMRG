About spandan


Lights. Music. Competition. Chaos. Creativity.

Spandan is the annual national-level fest of PIMR, Gwalior, where talent, creativity, competition, music and celebration come together. From exciting events to cultural performances and unforgettable moments, Spandan turns the campus into a celebration of energy and talent.
Spandan isn’t just an event on the calendar — it’s the experience that turns ordinary college days into unforgettable memories.
